Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

YAWR Services ("we", "our", or "us") operates the YAWR Timesheets mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal and operational data. By using this application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our administrative and tracking services, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Your full name, email address, assigned role (e.g., Staff, Admin, Senior Admin), and account status timestamps (creation, deactivation, and deletion requests).
  • Security Data: Passwords, which are strictly encrypted and securely hashed. We do not have access to your raw password.
  • Timesheet & Operational Data: Dates worked, logged hours, project and category selections, and descriptive narratives of your daily activities.
  • Expense & Mileage Data: Claimed mileage amounts, expense totals, and specific location/postcode details provided to verify travel and expenses.
  • Client & Program Data: Depending on the project (e.g., Advocacy, Direct Payments, Drop-In sessions), you may be required to log specific interactions. This can include client names, dates of birth, contact numbers, postcodes, ethnicities, and support outcomes. This data is strictly bound by our confidentiality policies and is collected solely for tracking organisational outputs.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data collected through the YAWR Timesheets app is never sold or used for marketing purposes. It is strictly used for:

  • Verifying and exporting staff working hours and expenses for administrative and payroll processing.
  • Auditing project outputs, service delivery, and community support metrics.
  • Ensuring compliance with our internal safeguarding and operational policies.

3. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

Your data is securely hosted using Supabase utilising PostgreSQL databases. All data transmitted between the app and our database is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/TLS) and encrypted at rest. We implement Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure that users can only access data appropriate for their permission level.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We comply with GDPR and local data protection regulations regarding your right to be forgotten for YAWR Timesheets (developed by Dawud Malik):

  • In-App Deletion: You may request the deletion of your account at any time via the Settings page within the app.
  • Manual Deletion Request: If you do not have the app installed, are locked out, or wish to request deletion from the web, email your request to yawrtimesheets@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request".
  • The Deletion Process: Upon requesting deletion, your account enters a mandatory grace period (minimum 7 days) allowing you to finalise any outstanding timesheets. Following a minimum of 30 days post-request, your personal identifying information (PII) will be permanently anonymised.
  • Retained Records: While your account will be anonymised, the numerical data you generated (such as total hours worked, projects supported, and expenses claimed) will be retained indefinitely as anonymous financial and administrative records required for legal and auditing compliance.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, or need administrative support, please contact YAWR Services administration at yawrmobiles@gmail.com.
